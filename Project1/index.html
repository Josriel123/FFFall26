Welcome to the Prehistoric Era

Dinosaurs ruled the Earth for over 160 million years during the Mesozoic Era. They lived in all kinds of environments, from warm prehistoric swamps and giant fern forests to dry sandy deserts. Some were small and fast, while others were as tall as four-story buildings.

This guide covers the major dinosaur time periods, some of the most famous species discovered by paleontologists, and interactive tools to test how you compare to these ancient creatures.

The Three Dinosaur Eras

The Age of Dinosaurs is divided into three major time periods:

252 - 201 MYA

Triassic Period

Climate: Hot and dry deserts

All land was connected into one giant continent called Pangea. The first small dinosaurs began to evolve alongside early reptiles and seed ferns.

201 - 145 MYA

Jurassic Period

Climate: Warm and rainy

Pangea started breaking apart, bringing rainfall and lush forests. Giant plant-eating dinosaurs like Brachiosaurus reached their largest sizes here.

145 - 66 MYA

Cretaceous Period

Climate: Mild with flowering plants

The final and most famous period. Apex predators like the T-Rex and horned dinosaurs like Triceratops thrived until the asteroid hit 66 million years ago.
