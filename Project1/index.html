Expedition Overview

Leo grew up on a small moon colony surrounded by telescopes and repair docks. Fascinated by ancient star charts and mechanical tinkering, he worked for years to restore a decommissioned yellow scout ship named the Starlight.

Today, Leo travels across distant planetary sectors to survey unexplored terrain, study rare minerals, and respond to emergency distress beacons. He is accompanied by Sparky, an autonomous robot dog built from recycled satellite parts who acts as his scout, geological sensor, and loyal companion.

Mission 01 Apr 14, 2025

The Blue Moon Rescue

Sector: Asteroid Belt Orion-9

Intercepted a damaged cargo vessel caught in an asteroid drift. Leo piloted the Starlight through heavy debris while Sparky secured the external towing latch to pull the crew to safety.

Mission 02 Sep 22, 2025

Finding the Crystal Cave

Sector: Planet Frost (Sub-Zero Ridge)

Descended into deep sub-surface glaciers to locate bioluminescent emerald crystals. The formations emit a stable electromagnetic pulse now studied for clean spacecraft energy.

Mission 03 Feb 18, 2026

The Sandstorm Escape

Sector: Martian Highlands

Trapped inside an unexpected iron-dust storm with zero visibility. Sparky utilized low-frequency radar pulses to guide the crew safely along a narrow canyon pass.

Mission 04 Jun 30, 2026

The Deep Crater Discovery

Sector: Lunar Rim Basin

Uncovered subterranean mineral water reserves sealed beneath millions of years of volcanic basalt. The site is now marked as a future refueling and biosphere outpost.
