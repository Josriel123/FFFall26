OWN-WORLD: Forest masthead, ivory text, a pale reading surface, and restrained ochre accents. Strong sans-serif headings and readable body text. Flat species figures, plain period dates, and simple controls.

FORM: Focused layout and typography work within the existing dinosaur-guide identity. No replacement-world roll or approved comp; code-led Read surface.

FINISH: Review the actual desktop and mobile captures, test every control, verify the supplied rubric, and document the finished Project1 design separately from the root page.
